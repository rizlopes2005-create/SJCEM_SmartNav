ABOUT SMARTNAV

Campus navigation, prototype edition.

This browser-only demo shows the core idea: campus places connected by walkable paths, with Dijkstra routing, live GPS tracking, QR start points, and an accessible route option.

The included map and distances are sample data based on the SJCEM campus sketch. Confirm the real locations and connections before use for real directions.
